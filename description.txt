A terraria mod based off of the entire Kirby series, integrating numerous elements from various games into Terraria!

This mod contains:
- Over 60 weapons from the series and original to the mod!
- Many enemies to find and fight!
- several familiar bosses spread throughout all of progression!
- Multiple materials and accessories to obtain!

Update 1.3 of 12/28/2025: Merry (late) Christmas!

It’s amazing how far this mod has come. Making it has been a very enriching experience! Thanks to all who took the time to indulge in it, whether that’s to play it or to help make it!

- Three new enemies! Including Batafire Mid-Boss for the Underworld and Jungle!
- Split rare stones into three types between each of the three Mid-Bosses, each type corresponding to a specific evo-weapon damage class, with recipes to help with conversion!
- Gave all the Mid-Bosses their own songs!
- Dark Matter has been re-tiered to pre-Golem rather than post-Golem!
- Gave significant changes to Kracko and Zero!
- Re-introduced the Dark Matter Rematch before Zero! Now with new attacks distinct from Dark Matter AND its own music track!
- Zero’s Eye is now a part of Zero on all difficulties!
- Introduced many decorations and vanity items!
- Introduced Soul Matter as a general pre-Lunar Events material! All 3rd tier evo-weapons, Triple Star and Rainbow Sword now require it!
- Finally implemented Birdon Feathers into the mod, and with it 2 recipes for new stuff!
- 5 new weapons!
- 3 new mounts!
- Revamped music for Power Orb and Zero!
- New music for Dark Matter's 2nd phase!
- Menu music!
- Achievements!
